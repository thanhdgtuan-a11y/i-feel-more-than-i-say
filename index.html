What is this

I FEEL MORE THAN I SAY. is a participatory graphic design project by a team of three design students. It gathers what young adults feel but leave unsaid in everyday situations — relationships and the self — and gives those words a place to be read.

How the words travel

  1. 01Paper reflection cards, four situations
  2. 02Anonymised, with separate consent to publish
  3. 03Transcribed, tagged and curated
  4. 04Posters, web reveals, this archive

Status & privacy

Only consented, anonymous responses are published. Reflecting, seeing other perspectives and finding words are design intentions — not proven results. Sample content is always labelled.
